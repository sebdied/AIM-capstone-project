The dataset contains 15 columns describing hourly environmental and air-quality measurements collected from gas sensors.
It includes date and time fields, several pollutant concentration measurements (such as CO, benzene, NMHC, NOx, and NO₂),
and sensor response variables that represent the output of different air-quality monitoring sensors. The dataset also records
environmental conditions including temperature, relative humidity, and absolute humidity.

Most pollutant and sensor measurements are numeric, while the Date and Time fields are stored as text values representing
the timestamp of each observation. A special value of -200 appears in multiple columns and indicates missing or erroneous
sensor readings.
